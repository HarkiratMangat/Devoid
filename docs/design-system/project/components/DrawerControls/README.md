Panels are drawers summoned at the table's edge and opened beside what they affect. A drawer overlays and never shrinks the artwork you are judging; only a tooltip or a drawer carries the `float` shadow.

- Every control is tri-state. It reads `auto · <value>` until deliberately taken over, because the engine applies its recommendation only where an option was left at its default. A UI that sends every flag turns the tool's thinking off.
- A control exposes an outcome ("Keep the fade"), never an engine flag. The person never types or reads a flag name.
- Rows are separated by a dashed `score` rule. The drawer heading is `label` at 600.
- Tab buttons carry a Pencil mark; the label appears on hover and focus, never rotated. The open tab is `raise` with a `cyan` inset ring and a 3px left bar.
- The consumer supplies the heading, the rows and the current values.
