## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored, but not yet composed.** The character is real and it is applied ONTO a category-standard shell rather than organising it.

Three moments could belong to no other product: the empty table's accretion horizon; the answer bar pinned under its own half of the seam; and the two-sided ledger that prices both answers before you pick. Everything holding them is interchangeable — strip the artwork out and what remains is the asset-manager shell any media tool ships.

The damning part: the generic half is winning. The specific parts are either inert (the seam shows no visible difference in every capture) or exiled (the ledger sits ~600px below the buttons it exists to inform).

**Deterministic scan:** 7 findings across `web/`, exit 2, no DEGRADED banner. One is the accepted `repeating-stripes-gradient`. The other six are all in `web/advice.js` — five `design-system-color`, one `design-system-radius` — and none is a false positive. Three stale rgba fallbacks in the same file are invisible to the rule, which only matches hex.

**Visual overlays:** not attempted. The repo bans browser panes; substituted the ten real-Electron captures. No user-visible overlay exists.

## What's Working

1. **The honesty architecture, implemented rather than asserted.** `data-single` collapses the wipe rather than labelling an uncut source "cut". The film head says "the comparison shows one frame". `not checked` gets a mark and equal typographic weight.
2. **The two-sided ledger.** `keep it 52,011 / 15,589 survive` against `cut it 54,058 / 13,542`. It converts an unanswerable question about intent into a priced comparison.
3. **Blocked-then-queue.** Press Cut with an unanswered asset and it queues, says how many are going now, and offers "Take me to it" which opens the asset and focuses the first answer button.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The hatch is painted over the exact rectangle the seam exists to reveal

`web/app.css:897` · `web/app.js:95, 1598`. VERIFIED: `.qregion` carries no `clip-path`; only `.wipe .after` is clipped. So the overlay paints identically on both halves and structurally cannot differ across the seam. Compounding it, the seam opens at a hardcoded 50% with no relation to the disputed bbox.

A person drags the cut line and nothing changes, in either direction, at the exact moment the product's thesis is on trial. `seamCanHelp` exists to prevent precisely this and is being defeated by an overlay drawn above it.

**Fix:** while a group is unanswered and on the seam, draw outline + tag only — drop both background layers. Open the seam at the horizontal centre of the disputed bbox.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

### [P1] The decision is scattered across four zones with its evidence exiled

`web/index.html:83-141` · `web/app.js:655, 734`. Question heading top-right, "answer it under the seam", buttons bottom-left, submit back top-right, numbers bottom-right ~600px below the heading. Eye travel for one binary: right → left → right → down.

The `Answer` button is the loudest control in the column and is not the decision — it is a second commit step behind it, disabled until the decision is made.

**Fix:** move the ledger directly under the answer pair; delete the `Answer` button, since `noteAnswer` commits on pick and ⌘Z already undoes.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

### [P1] The disabled primary is unreadable, and the contrast gate is blind to it

`web/app.css:582` · `scripts/check_contrast.py:76`. Measured from the captured window: emitting `#FFFFFF` on `#A6C5CF` = **1.82:1**; collapsed = **3.00:1**. The gate tests only the ENABLED pair, so `check:contrast` passes green while the shipped pixel fails — and the button is disabled for the entire time the question is open.

**Fix:** explicit disabled token pair measured in both states, added to `check_contrast.py`.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### [P1] Eight undifferentiated region tools, keep and cut separated by colour alone

`web/index.html:124` · `web/canvas.js`. One row, identical geometry and weight, distinguished by a tinted left border. In greyscale Keep and Cut are indistinguishable — the precise failure DESIGN.md declares impossible, uncaught because `check:greyscale` covers states, not controls.

**Fix:** two labelled groups with a divider, each button carrying its grease-pencil mark.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P1] `web/advice.js` carries a whole stale palette generation

Six detector findings plus three the rule cannot see. `#E7EDEB` and `#9DAEAA` are a previous generation of `--graphite`/`--graphite-2`; `--score-2`, `--mark` and `--score` fallbacks carry `.19`/`.34`/`.09` against the live `.42`/`.62`/`.16`. All are the fallback half of `var(--token, …)` pairs and every token is defined, so none paints — except `border-radius:5px`, which is unconditional and off the scale.

**Fix:** delete the fallbacks. A `var()` fallback for a token that always exists is a second palette nobody maintains.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

## Questions to Consider
